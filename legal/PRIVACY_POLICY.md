# Privacy Policy: Wedding Cannon: Shaadi Chaos

**Effective date:** 4 November 2026
**Developer:** {{DEVELOPER_NAME}} ("we", "us")
**Contact:** {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}

This policy explains what information *Wedding Cannon: Shaadi Chaos* (the "Game") handles when you play it on our website, on itch.io, on CrazyGames, on Poki or on YouTube Playables.

## 1. Summary
- We do **not** collect, sell or share your personal information.
- The Game has **no accounts, no chat, no user-generated content and no in-app purchases**.
- Your progress is saved **on your own device**, or in the **cloud save of the platform you play on**.
- When you play on a gaming platform, **that platform and its advertising partners** may collect data under their own privacy policies (see section 4).

## 2. Information the Game stores
The Game saves a small file of game progress so you can continue where you left off:
- levels cleared, stars and best scores
- Endless and Daily Challenge records and your day streak
- your chosen outfit
- settings (volume, voice, vibration, reduce effects)

This data contains **no name, email, location, contact list, photos or device identifiers**. Where it is stored:
- **Our website and itch.io:** in your browser's local storage on your device. We never receive it. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data, or with *Settings → Reset progress*.
- **CrazyGames:** in the CrazyGames cloud save (SDK data module), so progress follows your CrazyGames account, with a copy in local storage.
- **YouTube Playables:** in YouTube's cloud save for Playables (`saveData`), linked to your YouTube account by YouTube.
- **Poki:** in your browser's local storage.

## 3. Information we do NOT collect
- **No analytics or tracking.** The Game counts events such as "level cleared" only in temporary memory on your device, to tune the game during development. These counts are never sent to us or to anyone else.
- **No cookies.** The Game itself sets none.
- **No microphone or camera.** The Game never accesses either.
- **No location.** The Game never accesses your location.
- **Voice commentary** uses your device's built-in text-to-speech, which runs on your device. No audio is recorded.
- **Vibration** uses your phone's vibration motor and sends no data anywhere.

## 4. Gaming platforms and advertising
If you play on **CrazyGames**, **Poki** or **YouTube**, the platform hosts the Game and may show advertisements between levels. If you choose to, it may also show a rewarded ad in exchange for an extra launch. The platform and its advertising partners may collect information, such as device data, IP address and ad interactions, and may use cookies, under their own privacy policies. We do not control or receive this data. Please read:
- CrazyGames privacy policy: on crazygames.com
- Poki privacy policy: on poki.com
- Google / YouTube privacy policy: on policies.google.com

The website and itch.io versions show **no ads**.

## 5. Children
The Game is a cartoon, all-ages casual game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Gaming platforms apply their own child-safety and ad rules (for example, COPPA in the US).

## 6. Your rights
Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Your saved progress is on your device or in your platform account, and you can delete it yourself as described above. For data held by a platform, contact that platform.

This policy is intended to be consistent with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the EU/UK GDPR and the US COPPA.

## 7. Security
The Game runs entirely in your browser:
- Its web build uses a Content-Security-Policy that blocks outside scripts and network calls.
- Saved data is checked field by field when it is loaded.

## 8. Changes
If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version where the Game is listed.
